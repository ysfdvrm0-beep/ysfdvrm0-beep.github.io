The Artist

Yusuf Devrim Işık is a DJ and electronic music artist focused on creating immersive and energetic musical experiences.


His sound moves between melodic techno, deep house, afro house and carefully selected Turkish edits. Energy, atmosphere and emotion are at the center of every set.
